# PhantomTabs

PhantomTabs is a productivity-focused Chrome Extension designed to help users understand and improve their browsing habits. It tracks daily website usage, provides detailed statistics and insights, and includes a Focus Mode to minimize distractions during work or study sessions.

## Features

🚀 **Daily Website Tracking**

* Monitor the websites you visit throughout the day.

📊 **Usage Statistics**

* View detailed analytics and time spent on each website.

🎯 **Focus Mode**

* Stay productive by reducing distractions and focusing on important tasks.

📈 **Usage Insights**

* Understand browsing patterns and identify time-consuming activities.

⚡ **Lightweight & Easy to Use**

* Clean interface with real-time tracking and reporting.

## Why PhantomTabs?

Most people spend hours online without realizing where their time goes. PhantomTabs helps users gain visibility into their digital habits, make informed decisions, and build a more productive browsing experience.
